About Me

Hi👋, I'm Rahmat Abdulfattah, a Front-End Developer based in West Africa. I started my coding journey in 2020, diving into the world of web development with a focus on creating visually appealing, user-friendly websites.

Over the past few years, I have honed my skills in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Jekyll, Tailwind CSS and Bootstrap. Whether I'm building responsive websites with modern frameworks or optimizing web applications for performance, I am always learning and adapting to new technologies.

Besides my front-end development expertise, I am also proficient in Adobe Suite, which I use for graphic design and branding work. As a freelance brand designer, I have helped businesses create compelling visual identities that resonate with their target audiences.

I also have experience in building WordPress sites, giving me a well-rounded understanding of both static and dynamic web platforms.

In addition to my professional skills, I am also a proud mom, which helps me manage my time effectively and stay organized, both in my work and personal life.

My mission is to build functional, beautiful, and accessible websites that make a positive impact. If you’re looking for someone who can bring your vision to life, feel free to reach out.
